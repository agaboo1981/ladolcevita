Our Legacy

Crafted with Passion

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there parking available?

Yes, we offer complimentary valet parking for all our guests during dinner hours. There is also a public parking garage just one block away.

Do you have a dress code?

We recommend smart casual attire. While we don't require jackets, we want our guests to feel comfortable in an elegant dining environment.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We offer gluten-free pasta options and our chefs are happy to adapt many dishes to accommodate allergies or vegan preferences.

Do you host private events?

Yes, we have a private dining room that can accommodate up to 30 guests. Please contact us for details.
