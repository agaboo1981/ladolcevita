A trattoria inside the walls.

Six nights a week we cook the food of this city: tortelli, farro, buccellato, and the wine of the hills you can see from the tower.

Tortelli, made this morning.

Rolled by hand at two o'clock, filled with ricotta and herbs, finished with butter the colour of the hills at sunset.

Before you eat, walk. The whole of Lucca is inside four kilometres of wall, and we are at the middle of it.

One evening, hour by hour.

Two o'clock

The pasta is rolled by hand. Tortelli first, this city's own shape.

Five o'clock

The first tables. The candles are lit one at a time, never all at once.

Seven o'clock

The pass fills. Every plate is looked at before it leaves the kitchen.

Eight o'clock

The room at its loudest and happiest. Two pans on the fire at once.

Nine o'clock

Something red from the hills, by the glass or the bottle. Ask and we will pour you the right one.

Ten o'clock

Tiramisu and espresso, and nobody in any hurry to leave.
